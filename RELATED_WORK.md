# Related Work

This paper is an independent companion in the WRRA cosmology sequence.

- **WRRA Core** defines the domain agnostic execution architecture and validation rules. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22650956](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22650956)
- **Twist Stress Universe and the Mass Phenotype of Dark Matter** develops the dark mass interpretation and cross scale acceleration closure. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22700557](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22700557)
- **WRRA Spatial Twist Selection of Galactic Disk Planes and the Dark Mass Phenotype** separates angular momentum from plane selection and applies the state to a Milky Way toy model. DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23003875](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23003875)

The present work moves upstream. Its distinct contribution is the explicit separation of finite closure, topology, curvature, distributed local twist, accumulated holonomy, twist stress, expansion, and redshift. Möbius is used as a minimal analogy for twist accumulated across a closed form, not as one cut or as a literal cosmic topology. The paper also defines pure expansion, pure twist, and hybrid model spaces under one preregistered validation architecture.
