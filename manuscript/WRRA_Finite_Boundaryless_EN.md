## Abstract

This paper formulates a new upstream cosmological hypothesis within the Wonsik Reality Renderer Architecture. The starting intuition is that physical reality should not require an actually infinite stored state. A finite universe need not possess an edge, however, and finiteness alone does not select a unique topology or produce gravity. The model therefore makes a more specific and falsifiable choice: a distributed local twist can accumulate along transport and leave a nontrivial global holonomy whose local residues enter gravitational dynamics even when the corresponding information is not rendered as visible matter. A Möbius strip supplies only the simplest visual example of a twist distributed continuously around a closed form. It is not a single physical cut or seam and is not proposed as the literal topology of the universe because it has a boundary and is two dimensional.

The paper separates three claims that are often conflated. Compactness concerns global extent, curvature concerns local geometry, and holonomy concerns the residue obtained by transporting a state around a closed path. A flat three torus is compact and boundaryless while remaining locally flat, so topology alone cannot explain excess gravity. WRRA requires an explicit causal chain from global identification to holonomy, from holonomy to a local twist residue, and from that residue to an effective stress tensor. The minimal bookkeeping target is

$$
\mathcal C\longrightarrow A_T\longrightarrow\mathcal H\longrightarrow\Theta\longrightarrow(\rho_T,q_T)\longrightarrow T^{T}_{\mu\nu}\longrightarrow\mathcal O.
$$

Cosmic expansion and twist evolution are treated as independent state variables. The spatial metric is parameterized as $g_{ij}=a^2\widetilde g_{ij}(\Theta)$, allowing expansion only, twist only, and hybrid histories. The exact photon redshift remains $1+z=(u_\mu k^\mu)_e/(u_\mu k^\mu)_o$. A proposed factorization, $1+z_{\mathrm{obs}}=(a_0/a_e)e^{\Xi_T}$, is a conditional hypothesis rather than a derived result. It becomes scientific only when $\Xi_T$ is frozen by the same twist state that also determines galactic additional gravity, disk plane geometry, warps, vertical equilibrium, and lensing.

At galactic scale the proposal connects to an existing WRRA Milky Way model in which the unoriented director $[n]$ selects a local disk plane but does not define angular momentum. The magnitude and anisotropic sectors $(\rho_T,q_T)$ generate a candidate dark mass phenotype. The present work does not provide a complete covariant action or a cosmological likelihood. It provides a disciplined model space, an ownership map for every equation, and a preregistered falsification program spanning supernova time dilation, distance duality, Tolman dimming, BAO, the CMB, redshift drift, structure growth, lensing, galaxy dynamics, vertical equilibrium, and Solar System limits.

## 1 Research Question and Main Conclusion

The research question is not simply whether a finite universe is mathematically possible. Compact boundaryless spatial manifolds are already standard mathematical possibilities. The question is whether a finite globally closed state can leave a dynamically reusable residue that explains more than one observational sector.

The main conclusion is conditional. A global twist is not implied by finiteness, and gravitational stress is not implied by topology. A viable WRRA theory must add a dynamical map from global transport to local stress and must freeze that map before confronting multiple data sets. If the same state $\Theta$ accounts for a component of cosmological redshift and for the additional gravitational and geometric outputs of galaxies, the proposal is more than a relabeling of expansion or dark matter. If separate arbitrary functions are introduced for each observation, the proposal collapses into an unfalsifiable reparameterization.

This paper therefore presents a structural hypothesis, not a completed replacement for general relativity or the standard cosmological model. Its central object is accumulated twist, not a one time topological flip at a privileged section.

Let the total physically available state be decomposed as

$$
D_{\mathrm{total}}=D_{\mathrm{rendered}}+D_{\mathrm{residual}}.
$$

$$
M_{\mathrm{visible}}=\Pi_M(D_{\mathrm{rendered}}).

The central hypothesis is that a state need not be optically or materially rendered in order to remain dynamically present. Gravitational bookkeeping may therefore require

The rejection of actual infinity motivates a finite model but does not determine whether the universe is spatially closed, temporally recurrent, or both. Those are additional model choices. The present proposal selects a compact boundaryless spatial sector with possible nontrivial holonomy as the minimal research target.

## 3 Finite Boundaryless and Accumulated Twist Closure

A finite interval has endpoints, but a circle is finite in length and has no endpoint. Likewise, a sphere is finite in area and has no boundary. In three dimensions a three sphere or a compact quotient such as a three torus can be finite and boundaryless. The phrase finite but boundaryless is therefore mathematically coherent without invoking a physical edge.

The Möbius strip is useful because its half twist is distributed around the whole band. No physical section is privileged as the place where the twist occurs. The important intuition is therefore not one cut followed by one identification, nor merely a single orientation flip after a circuit. It is that infinitesimal local twisting can accumulate into a finite global state. The strip is still not an admissible literal model of cosmic space because it is a two dimensional surface with a boundary. The cosmological hypothesis instead concerns a higher dimensional compact manifold or bundle with a distributed connection.

Let $\omega_T$ denote a local twist rate along a path. The accumulated open path twist is

$$
\Theta_\gamma=\int_\gamma\omega_T.
$$

For a connection $A_T$, closed transport produces the ordered holonomy

$$
\mathcal H(\gamma)=\mathcal P\exp\left(\oint_\gamma A_T\right).
$$

If transport repeats while the state evolves, the historical accumulation is

$$
\mathcal H_N(\gamma)=\mathcal H_N\mathcal H_{N-1}\cdots\mathcal H_1.
$$

In an Abelian approximation the corresponding twist phases add, $\Theta_N=\sum_{m=1}^{N}\Theta_m$. In a non Abelian model the order of the local increments matters. A static Möbius orientation flip is only a finite order special case and is too simple to represent the proposed accumulating cosmic state. The field $\Theta(t,\mathbf x)$ denotes the effective local record of this accumulated transport. A nonidentity value $\mathcal H(\gamma)\neq I$ means that a transported state returns with a residue, but the proposed physics resides in the distributed connection and its accumulated history rather than in an isolated end point identification.

Three distinctions are essential.

| Concept | Question answered | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Compactness | Is the total spatial extent finite | Local curvature or stress |
| Curvature | Does local parallel transport depend on geometry | Nontrivial global identification |
| Holonomy | What accumulated transformation remains after closed transport | A unique stress tensor without dynamics |

A flat three torus supplies the decisive counterexample. It is compact and boundaryless and can be locally Euclidean. Thus global closure alone cannot be identified with dark matter, dark energy, or curvature.

The scale factor $a(t)$ controls isotropic volume scaling. The dimensionless state $\Theta$ controls shape, gluing, transport, or anisotropic deformation. This parameterization defines three nested cases.

## 6 Photon Redshift

The exact general relativistic definition of observed redshift is

$$
1+z_{\mathrm{obs}}=\frac{(u_\mu k^\mu)_e}{(u_\mu k^\mu)_o},
$$

where $k^\mu$ is the photon wave vector and $u^\mu$ is the emitter or observer four velocity. In a homogeneous FLRW spacetime this reduces to $1+z=a_0/a_e$.

WRRA proposes a conditional factorization

$$
1+z_{\mathrm{obs}}=(1+z_{\mathrm{exp}})(1+z_T)=\frac{a_0}{a_e}\exp(\Xi_T),
$$

where $\Xi_T$ is a line of sight transport functional derived from $\Theta$ and the photon path. A schematic local form is

$$
\Xi_T[\gamma]=\int_\gamma \mathcal K_T\bigl(\Theta,\nabla\Theta,\dot\Theta,k^\mu,u^\mu\bigr)d\lambda.
$$

At low redshift the logarithmic rates approximately add,

$$
H_{\mathrm{obs}}\simeq H_{\mathrm{exp}}+H_T.
$$

This is not yet a prediction because the kernel $\mathcal K_T$ has not been derived from a frozen action. If $\Xi_T(z)$ is fitted independently for every distance indicator, the construction has no falsifying power. The decisive requirement is that the same $\Theta$ and the same kernel reproduce redshift observables while also fixing nonredshift phenomena.

## 11 Cosmological Closure Tests

### 11.1 Supernova Time Dilation

Observed Type Ia supernova light curves broaden approximately by $1+z$. A twist redshift law must transform emission times and photon frequencies consistently, not merely shift spectral lines. Failure to reproduce the same temporal dilation falsifies a pure twist redshift mechanism.

### 11.2 Distance Duality and Tolman Dimming

For metric light propagation with photon number conservation,

$$
d_L=(1+z)^2d_A.
$$

The same null propagation law must also reproduce surface brightness dimming proportional to $(1+z)^{-4}$. A kernel chosen only to match redshift but inconsistent with luminosity distance, angular diameter distance, or Tolman dimming is rejected.

### 11.3 Baryon Acoustic Oscillations

Radial BAO constrains an expansion related rate, while transverse BAO constrains an angular diameter distance. A hybrid theory must calculate both from the same metric and photon transport rule. It cannot assign radial BAO to twist and transverse BAO to expansion with unrelated functions.

### 11.4 Cosmic Microwave Background

The model must account for the near blackbody spectrum, the temperature redshift relation $T(z)=T_0(1+z)$ or a precisely derived alternative, acoustic angular scales, damping, polarization, and lensing. Existing searches have not detected a compact topology with a fundamental domain smaller than roughly the diameter of the last scattering surface. This constrains small compact models but does not prove that the universe is infinite.

### 11.5 Redshift Drift

The time derivative of source redshift provides a direct separation test because expansion and evolving transport need not predict the same drift. In the proposed factorization,

$$
\frac{d}{dt_0}\ln(1+z_{\mathrm{obs}})=
\frac{d}{dt_0}\ln\left(\frac{a_0}{a_e}\right)+\frac{d\Xi_T}{dt_0}.
$$

A frozen $\Xi_T$ evolution therefore gives a specific correction to the Sandage Loeb signal.

### 11.6 Growth and Lensing

The effective stress that changes galaxy dynamics must enter cosmological perturbations and light deflection consistently. The same metric potentials must confront weak lensing, redshift space distortions, cluster dynamics, and the matter power spectrum. Arbitrary gravitational slip introduced only after a lensing mismatch counts as a new model degree of freedom.

## 12 Galactic Closure Tests

The galactic sector supplies a nearer and more structured test bench.

First, one frozen acceleration scale and response must be evaluated across complete rotation curve samples, including residual correlations and uncertainty propagation. Second, the same stress state must calculate vertical velocity dispersion, disk thickness, and flaring through the vertical Jeans equation. Third, the same metric must reproduce gravitational lensing. Fourth, one evolving plane field should relate stellar, atomic gas, and molecular gas warp geometries and vertical motions. Fifth, Solar System constraints must be recovered without an object specific screening function added after the fact.

Polar rings, counterrotating disks, strongly interacting galaxies, and low surface brightness systems are especially informative because they separate plane selection, rotation direction, environmental torque, and additional gravity.

## 15 Falsification Conditions

The common twist hypothesis is rejected or sharply weakened if any of the following occurs after the model is frozen.

1. The redshift kernel matches spectra but fails supernova time dilation, distance duality, Tolman dimming, BAO, or CMB temperature scaling.
2. A single $\Theta$ evolution cannot fit cosmological distances and redshift drift without a new function for each probe.
3. The stress needed for rotation curves fails gravitational lensing, structure growth, clusters, or Solar System constraints.
4. The plane director required for warps is unrelated to the stress magnitude required for additional gravity.
5. Galaxy by galaxy values of $a_T$ or unrelated plane fields are required after the universal state is declared.
6. No conserved and stable covariant completion can realize the proposed effective stress.
7. The model reduces exactly to FLRW through $a_{\mathrm{eff}}=F(\Theta)$ and produces no independently calculated non FLRW observable.

These conditions are intentionally stronger than reproducing one rotation curve. The theory claims a common owner, so it must survive common ownership tests.

**Stage four  Preregistered validation**  Freeze parameters on a declared training set, publish hashes and thresholds, and score held out cosmological and galactic data. The result should be reported even if negative.

## 17 Conclusion

The motivating idea can be expressed rigorously without claiming more than has been shown. A finite universe does not need an edge. A Möbius strip provides a minimal picture of twist distributed around a closed form, but it is not the universe and it does not imply a privileged cosmic cut. Finiteness does not imply twist, and twist does not imply gravity. WRRA therefore adds a testable chain from global closure through a distributed connection, accumulated holonomy, and transport residue to effective stress and observables.

The proposal's most important move is to separate expansion from twist. The universe may expand, its twist may deepen, or both may occur. The observed redshift is then a product rather than an informal sum, while low redshift logarithmic rates may add. The model becomes distinct from standard cosmology only when the same frozen twist state also calculates additional gravity, disk plane geometry, warp, vertical response, lensing, and growth.

Visible mass is interpreted as a rendered phenotype, while residual state may remain gravitationally active. That interpretation is currently a causal hypothesis. The existing Milky Way calculation shows that a signless plane field and a stress magnitude sector can be kept conceptually separate from angular momentum and can be placed in one auditable toy model. The next decisive step is a covariant action and a joint cosmological galactic likelihood. Until then, the correct status is a structured, quantitative, and falsifiable research program.
