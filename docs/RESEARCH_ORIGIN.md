# Research Origin

The conceptual image behind this paper arose while Wonsik Choi was viewing **KOO JEONG A: OUSSSMOS** at Leeum Museum of Art in Seoul. The exhibition was presented in M2 from 5 September to 27 December 2026.

The relevant intuition was not a single cut plane or a localized orientation flip. It was a twist distributed across a whole spatial relation and accumulated through traversal and history.

This provenance statement does not treat an artwork or exhibition as scientific evidence. It records the origin of the research question. The mathematical definitions, physical hypotheses, calculations, and falsification conditions remain the author's independent responsibility.
